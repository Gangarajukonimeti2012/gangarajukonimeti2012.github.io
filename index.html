Olist E-commerce Sales Lakehouse

Problem
An online store kept orders, customers, products and payments in separate files. Nobody could easily answer "how much did we sell each month?" or "which categories sell best?"
What I built
Raw CSVs flow through bronze, silver and gold layers on Databricks, all governed by Unity Catalog. Gold is a star schema with primary and foreign keys, and lineage is tracked automatically.
Result
A 112,650-row fact table and an AI/BI dashboard with 6 charts that answer the sales, category and delivery questions directly.
